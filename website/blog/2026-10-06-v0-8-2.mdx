A small release after [`v0.8.1`](/releases-updates/v0-8-1), from turning on MTA-STS hosting for real domains.

{/* truncate */}

## MTA-STS MX hosts come from DNS again

When the old Manage MTA-STS page became a tab on each domain, its button for looking up a domain's MX hosts didn't
come with it. So a domain switched on from **Advanced** could end up with no MX hosts, which means the policy dotMARC
served listed no mail servers at all, and senders that check MTA-STS could refuse to deliver.

* **Filled in from DNS.** When the MX hosts field is empty, it's filled in from the domain's MX records as soon as the
  MTA-STS tab opens, ready for you to check and save. **Look up from DNS** is back beside the field to fetch them again.
* **Never on without MX hosts.** dotMARC refuses to save MTA-STS as on with no MX hosts, and a bulk import skips such
  a row with a note instead.
* **Easy to spot.** A domain that's already on with no MX hosts says so on its MTA-STS tab, with the hosts filled in
  from DNS and the **Advanced** section opened.

**Check your domains after upgrading:** open the MTA-STS tab of each domain with hosting on. Any that shows the
warning needs its filled-in MX hosts saved.

## Waiting for a certificate isn't a failure

On Azure, a new domain's TLS certificate can take a few minutes to issue. dotMARC used to show that wait as
**Failed** with Azure's raw error, even though it carried on and finished by itself. It now shows **Waiting for
certificate**, with a note that Azure is still issuing it and dotMARC retries automatically. A certificate Azure
can't issue still shows as **Failed**, with Azure's reason.

## Help beside every setting

The settings pages now have a small help icon beside each field, explaining what it does and what to put there:
alert settings and the HaloPSA connection, DNS push (including the exact callback URL each provider needs), audit
log retention, and MTA-STS. Hover over it, or tap or tab to it.

## Upgrading

There are no database migrations and no new settings. Redeploy, then check your MTA-STS domains as above.
