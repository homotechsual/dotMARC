The biggest release since [`v0.8.2`](/releases-updates/v0-8-2): dotMARC now works with the PSA you use, gives your
clients a branded view of their own domains, and sends them a regular report.

{/* truncate */}

## ConnectWise and Autotask, alongside HaloPSA

Alert tickets are no longer HaloPSA only. dotMARC raises and closes tickets in
[ConnectWise PSA](/docs/psa/connectwise) and [Autotask](/docs/psa/autotask) too, and you can connect more than one PSA
at once.

* **Manage > PSA settings** has a tab for each PSA, with a **Test the integration** button that creates, checks and
  closes a real ticket.
* Each Group (and, where needed, each domain) links to a company in each PSA. Manage groups suggests matches by name,
  and a domain import can set the company per PSA.
* dotMARC checks its open tickets regularly, so closing a ticket in any PSA resolves its alert. HaloPSA's webhook
  still works and stays the fastest route.
* Each alert shows the tickets raised for it in each PSA.

See [PSA integration](/docs/psa-integration) for how several PSAs work together.

## A branded client portal

Give a client contact a simple, read-only view of their own domains instead of your dashboard. Turn on **Client
portal** for their access grant (it must be limited to their Groups), and when they sign in at your usual dotMARC
address they see:

* **Your domains:** each domain's status in plain words (Protected, Monitoring only, Needs attention, No reports yet),
  its pass rate and trend, and open alerts.
* **A page per domain:** what its policy does, its health checks, who sends as it, and its alerts.

**Manage > Branding** sets your product name, colours, logos and support details, with a contrast warning and a live
preview in light and dark. On **Manage groups**, a Group can override the name, logos and colours with the client's
own, and **Preview as client** shows exactly what they'll see. Portal users can't reach anything else in dotMARC, or
the API. See [Client portal](/docs/client-portal).

## Scheduled client reports

dotMARC can now send each client a branded PDF report weekly, monthly or quarterly, by email:

* A cover with their status in one line, a summary of every domain with the change since last time, a pass rate
  trend, each domain's policy, health, top senders and what receivers did, and a short list of what to do next.
* Set up email on **Manage > Email & reports**, through Microsoft Graph or any SMTP server, along with the time zone,
  send hour and number format reports use.
* Each Group's **Reports** button on Manage groups sets its schedule and recipients, and can send or download a report
  for any past period, such as a quarter for a business review.
* A report that can't be sent is retried for a day, then raises a **Client report failed** alert.

See [Client reports](/docs/client-reports).

## Alerts in Slack

Alerts can go to a Slack channel through an incoming webhook, alongside Teams and the generic webhook. Each channel on
**Alert settings** now has its own switch and **Send test** button.

## Upgrading

Database migrations run automatically when the new version starts.

* **HaloPSA moves to PSA settings.** Your HaloPSA connection, defaults and client links carry over to
  **Manage > PSA settings**. Nothing to redo.
* **Client reports through Microsoft Graph** need the `Mail.Send` application permission on dotMARC's app
  registration. A report too big for one Graph request (over about 3 MB, so only with a very large logo) also needs
  `Mail.ReadWrite` in place of `Mail.Read`. With SMTP, neither is needed. Reports stay off until you set up email.
* **Autotask** needs an API tracking identifier from your own Autotask setup until dotMARC has its own.
* **A new permission, ReportsManage,** covers email settings and client reports. The Admin role gets it automatically;
  add it to any custom role that should manage reports.
